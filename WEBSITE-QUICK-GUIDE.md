## Keep it presentable

- **Match the photo to the frame.** Wide banners need landscape photos; homepage service cards need a square (1:1) crop; Services page cards use a slightly wide 5:4 frame. Wrong shapes look stretched or cut off.
- **Keep text the right length.** Hero text and card summaries should stay short (a few lines). If a section looks crowded after saving, shorten the wording instead of forcing long paragraphs into a small space.
- **Use clear, professional photos.** Prefer sharp, well-lit images. Avoid blurry phone shots and heavy filters that clash with the site’s calm clinical look.
- **Feature your best content first.** When the dashboard offers featured slots (certificates, cases, events, gallery), choose the strongest items so visitors see them on the first screen.
- **Remove clutter.** Delete outdated, empty, or placeholder cards so the site stays clean and trustworthy.
- **Always preview on phone and desktop.** Layouts differ by screen size — check both after important changes, and hard-refresh if an old image still appears.

## Taking photos for the website

- **Stand farther back.** Take a wider shot of the certificate, person, or room — not a tight close-up. Extra space around the subject makes cropping much easier later.
- **Leave room to edit.** Keep margins around logos, faces, and certificate edges so you can reframe without cutting important details.
- **Aim for even light and a level shot.** Soft, even lighting works best. Avoid strong tilt; a straighter photo needs less fixing in the editor.
- **Use the right file type.** Upload **JPG** or **PNG**. iPhone **HEIC/HEIF** files are not supported — convert them first.
- **Certificates:** Upload the photo → open **Edit photo** → optionally press **Improve photo** (Balanced is a good default) → drag/zoom to fit the frame → **Save**, then save the certificate.
- **Services:** Upload **one** photo, then frame it twice — **5:4** for the Services page card and **1:1** for the homepage side image — then press **Save service**.
